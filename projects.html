FBPINN Optimization Research

(PNNL)

Conducted research on optimizations for Fourier-based Physics-Informed Neural Networks (FBPINNs) during my internship at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory. I investigated compiler-level optimizations for FBPINNs, a specialized type of PINN that uses a series of overlapping domain decompositions to more accurately represent complex physics problems. My work focused on developing optimization techniques to improve the performance and efficiency of existing FBPINN implementations in scientific computing applications.

Double-Single Arithmetic Research

(UTEP)

Investigated the use of double-single arithmetic for systems with limited double precision support during my research assistantship at UTEP. I developed a custom compiler pass using LLVM that automatically transforms given programs to use double-single arithmetic, enabling higher precision computations on hardware with limited double precision capabilities.
